About me

Creative thinking, with a finance mindset.

I’m Saurabh Singh Prashar, a CA student with practical experience across digital marketing and finance. My experience has allowed me to work directly with clients, understand their requirements, plan content, coordinate campaigns and think about how a brand can communicate effectively in the digital space.

I have personally handled clients while working with Its One Media, a media company based in Delhi. This experience taught me that digital marketing is not simply about posting content — it is about understanding the audience, communicating the right message and continuously improving based on performance.

Alongside marketing, my interest and education in finance give me a different perspective. I naturally think about numbers, performance, business models and return on effort. I want to combine creativity with analytical thinking to build digital strategies that are not only attractive but also meaningful for a business.
